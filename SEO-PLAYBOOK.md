# SEO playbook for photoprint.io

## What the generator gives you
Unique data on every programmatic page (pixels, max sizes, PPI at common sizes, related links), the tool on every page,
canonical tags, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data, a sitemap, and internal links between cameras, sizes and business pages.

## Rules that keep pages indexable
1. Only publish pages that have different numbers and answers. Never add a page just to swap a keyword.
2. Release in waves (see `maxBatch`). Check Search Console after each wave before releasing the next.
3. Add cameras and sizes people actually search for. Check Google autocomplete for "largest print size for ...".
4. Do not add city or location pages until you have a partner who fulfils orders in that city.

## Write by hand (your money pages)
The 8 pages in `data/usecases.js` are starter drafts. Rewrite them with your own examples and photos, and aim for 15 to 30:
hotels, restaurants, offices, gyms, clinics, schools, retail, real estate staging, interior designers, event venues.
Add pages for wholesale labs: "wedding album printing for photographers", "fine art printing for photographers".
Add equipment pages once you have supplier partners.

## Get links and traffic in the first 90 days
- Make 3 to 5 YouTube videos using the checker (print size explainers) and link to the site.
- Share the tool with photography communities where self-promotion is allowed. Read each community's rules first.
- Message 20 interior designers and photographers on LinkedIn offering the free tool.
- Ask photography bloggers to link to the checker.

## Measure weekly (Search Console)
Pages indexed, impressions, clicks, and which queries show up. Improve pages with many impressions and low clicks (better title and description).

## Leads
Every request arrives by email with size, budget, timeline, country and page source. Keep them in a spreadsheet.
Only share a lead with a partner because the visitor ticked the consent box. Start with 10 to 20 leads for one partner as a pilot, and never sell one exclusive lead twice.
